Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Dayfax (“we”, “us”) operates the Dayfax mobile application and website at dayfax.in. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share personal information when you use our services in India.

1. Information we collect

  • Account & contact: mobile phone number, name, and OTP verification details.
  • Addresses: delivery addresses and related location details you provide.
  • Orders: cart contents, order history, payment status, and delivery status.
  • Device & usage: app version, device type, IP address, and basic logs needed for security and support.
  • Location (if enabled): approximate or precise location to show nearby stores and track deliveries.

2. How we use information

  • Create and manage your account, including OTP login.
  • Process orders, payments, refunds, and deliveries.
  • Communicate about orders, support, and important service updates.
  • Improve reliability, prevent fraud, and secure our systems.
  • Comply with applicable Indian laws and respond to lawful requests.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Delivery partners assigned to fulfil your order.
  • Payment processors and infrastructure providers who help run Dayfax.
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

4. Retention

We keep account, order, and related records for as long as needed to provide the service, meet legal/accounting requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion of your account subject to pending orders and legal obligations.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data (including HTTPS and access controls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Your choices

  • Update addresses and profile details in the app where available.
  • Control location permissions in your device settings.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your data.

7. Children

Dayfax is not directed at children under 18. If you believe a minor has provided personal data, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of Dayfax after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@dayfax.in or visit dayfax.in.
