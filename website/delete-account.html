Delete your DayFax account

Last updated: 16 September 2026

You can request deletion of your DayFax account by email. There is no fully automated self-serve deletion yet. You can also start this from the app: Profile → Delete account.

1. How to request deletion

  1. Email support@dayfax.in from your registered phone-linked contact or Google email.
  2. Use the subject line Account deletion request and include your registered phone number or account email so we can verify ownership.
  3. After we verify the request, we delete or anonymise personal data that is no longer required to provide the service.

Email account deletion request

2. What we delete

  • Account profile details (such as name and phone number linked to the account).
  • Saved delivery addresses tied to the account.
  • App session and login credentials associated with the account.
  • Other personal data that is not required for pending orders, refunds, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal/accounting obligations.

3. What we may retain

Some records may be kept where needed for pending orders, refunds, fraud prevention, disputes, or legal/accounting obligations under applicable Indian law. Retained data is limited to what is necessary and is not used for marketing.

4. Timing

We aim to process verified deletion requests within a reasonable time after ownership is confirmed, subject to open orders and legal retention requirements.

5. Privacy

For full details on how DayFax handles personal information, see our Privacy Policy.

6. Contact

Questions about account deletion: support@dayfax.in or visit dayfax.in.
